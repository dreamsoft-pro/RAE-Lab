# RAE-Lab: The Intelligence Observatory Manifest 🔬

## 🎯 Purpose
RAE-Lab is NOT a simple calculator for mathematical weights. It is the **Analytical Heart and Research Hub** of the RAE-Suite ecosystem. Its primary mission is to observe, analyze, and evolve the collective intelligence of the factory.

## 🧠 Core Pillars
1.  **Engineering Experimentation**: Every code generation (Phoenix) or command execution (Hive) is treated as a measurable experiment. Lab records the outcome, efficiency, and cost.
2.  **Kaizen Engine (Continuous Evolution)**: Lab identifies patterns of failure across modules. It is responsible for triggering self-refactoring of core contracts when logic gaps are detected.
3.  **Lean Econometrics**: Monitors token consumption and agent performance. It optimizes the "Value to Waste" ratio, ensuring that high-resource swarms are only used when necessary.
4.  **Reflective Synthesis**: Converts raw operational data into "Learned Lessons" for RAE-Memory, driving the long-term strategic growth of the factory.

## 📊 Strategic KPIs
*   **Success Rate First-Time**: Accuracy of the G-D-Q swarm.
*   **Muda (Waste) Index**: Identification of redundant agent calls.
*   **Evolution Velocity**: How fast the system adapts its Hard Contracts after a failure.

---
**Vision**: Autonomous Industrial Intelligence through Scientific Reflection.
